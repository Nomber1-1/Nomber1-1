## 📌 Selected work

- **[sportcenter](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/sportcenter)** — full-stack sports centre
  management: 69 REST endpoints, 8 JPA entities, 19 test classes. Where I learned that
  "it compiles" and "it's correct" are two different claims.
- **[gpt2-syntax-circuits](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/gpt2-syntax-circuits)** —
  mechanistic interpretability of subject-verb agreement in gpt2-small: ablation, ACDC
  circuit discovery, causal scrubbing. **90.67% faithfulness, 44.40% completeness** — the
  second number is the interesting one.
- **[walker-soccer-twos](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/walker-soccer-twos)** — humanoid
  ragdoll agents that learn to walk, then play 3v3 soccer. Zero goals in 530,000 training
  steps, and the write-up says so.
- **[os-simulator](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/os-simulator)** — an operating system in C:
  shell, multithreaded scheduler, demand paging. Published with its known bugs listed,
  because an interviewer finding them beats me hiding them.
