## My best work

- [sportcenter](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/sportcenter): full-stack sports centre management, 69 REST endpoints, 8 JPA entities, 19 test classes. It taught me that "it compiles" and "it's correct" are two different claims.
- [gpt2-syntax-circuits](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/gpt2-syntax-circuits): mechanistic interpretability of subject-verb agreement in gpt2-small, using ablation, ACDC circuit discovery and causal scrubbing. Faithfulness 90.67%, completeness 44.40%. The second number is the interesting one.
- [walker-soccer-twos](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/walker-soccer-twos): humanoid ragdoll agents that learn to walk, then play 3v3 soccer. They scored zero goals in 530,000 training steps, and the write-up says so.
- [os-simulator](https://github.com/Nomber1-1/os-simulator): an operating system in C, with a shell, a multithreaded scheduler and demand paging. I published it with the known bugs listed, because an interviewer finding them beats me hiding them.
